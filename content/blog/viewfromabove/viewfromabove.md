I sometimes think I'm just in it for the maps... **View from Above** is a visualisation side-project built on a fork of – and feeding back into – the incredible library [prettymaps](https://github.com/marceloprates/prettymaps). [prettymaps](https://github.com/marceloprates/prettymaps) uses freely-available [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=2/-41.2/-6.6) data and some custom [shapely](https://shapely.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) logic to make gorgeous birds-eye renders.

I've been working on making the visualisation more robust (it can struggle with bridges, large open space, etc.), adding [Blue Plaque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_plaque) indicators for my more nerdy friends, and generally creating cute gifts for friends and family.

If you'd like one for your meaningful neighbourhood, try out the original library (I believe it now ships with an app to make plotting easier) or get in [touch](/contact/)!
