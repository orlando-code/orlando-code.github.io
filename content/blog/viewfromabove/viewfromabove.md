**View from Above** is a mapping side-project built on a fork of [prettymaps](https://github.com/marceloprates/prettymaps), with a Streamlit front end for drawing customised maps from OpenStreetMap data.

- **Site:** [viewfromabove.streamlit.app](https://viewfromabove.streamlit.app/)
