I saw **Scenes from the Climate Era** (*SFTCE*) in a small theatre in West London. I left speechless. Once I'd organised my thoughts, I immediately reached out to David Finnigan, the writer. One thing led to another, and with David's generous permission, I put on the play in Cambridge in the famous <a href="https://www.adctheatre.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ADC Theatre</a>.

Leaving the show in London – its fifth international professional run after Sydney, Auckland, Manila, and Singapore – I felt that as many people as possible needed to see it. I'm grateful that I was able to share it with hundreds more in Cambridge, and hope to be involved with future productions!

I believe the power of the play comes from painting the scientific facts and data about climate change – past, present, and future – onto real peoples' lives. It makes the crisis (_era?_) more tangible, more immediate, and completely inescapable.

I'm rather proud of the website and publicity I created: check it out for yourself!

And if you're interested in putting the play on yourself, please <a href="https://davidfinig.com/scenes-from-the-climate-era/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">check out the official page</a> – and don't hesitate to get in touch.
